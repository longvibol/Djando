We use tag :%url%

1-HTML : 


<header id="main-navigation">
      <h1><a href=" {% url "starting-page" %}">Vibol' Blog</a></h1>
      <nav>
        <a href="">All Posts</a>
      </nav>
</header>


2-urls.py

urlpatterns = [
    path("", views.starting_page, name="starting-page"),
    path("posts", views.posts, name="posts-page"),
    path("post/<str:slug>", views.post_detail, name="post-detail-page"),
]


-----------------------


We route them by putting the name in the urls
